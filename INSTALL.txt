DEADLOCK RUNNER — QUICK INSTALL
================================

Requirements
------------
- Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer)
- macOS 14.6 or newer
- A Steam account with access to Deadlock
- Enough free space for Steam and Deadlock

Install
-------
1. Drag “Deadlock Runner.app” onto the Applications shortcut.
2. In Applications, Control-click Deadlock Runner and choose Open.
3. Approve macOS’s first-launch warning.
4. Wait while the official Steam installer runs and updates itself.
5. Sign in to Steam. Do not share your login or Steam Guard code.
6. Install Deadlock from your Steam Library.
7. Do not launch Deadlock yet. Quit Steam and the runner.
8. Reopen Deadlock Runner so it can patch the installed game and your
   per-account settings.
9. Launch Deadlock from Steam.

The runner automatically selects DirectX 11, applies safe Mac display
defaults when a Deadlock video file exists, and reapplies the known Xbox
controller fix whenever the runner starts.

If macOS will not open it
--------------------------
Try Control-click > Open first. This community build is ad-hoc signed, not
Apple-notarized. If macOS still says the app is damaged, open Terminal and
run this only after checking the downloaded SHA-256 file:

  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Deadlock Runner.app"

Then Control-click the app and choose Open again.

Troubleshooting
---------------
- Black screen: quit Steam and the runner, reopen Deadlock Runner, then try
  Deadlock again. Start in windowed mode before enabling fullscreen.
- Game update restored Vulkan: reopen the runner; it restores `-dx11`.
- Controller labels/input are wrong: reopen the runner, then reconnect the
  controller before launching Deadlock.
- Steam setup did not finish: quit the app and open it again.

Updating without losing Steam or Deadlock
-----------------------------------------
Steam, the game, and your settings are stored inside:

  Deadlock Runner.app/Contents/SharedSupport/prefix

Before replacing the app, quit Steam and the runner, Control-click the old
app, choose Show Package Contents, and copy that prefix folder somewhere
safe. Install the new app, replace its clean prefix folder with your backup,
then reopen Deadlock Runner. Keep the backup until everything launches.
