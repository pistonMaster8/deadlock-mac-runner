01

From download to game

  1. InstallOpen the DMG and drag Deadlock Runner into Applications.
  2. Open safelyControl-click the app, choose Open, and approve the macOS warning.
  3. Sign inThe official Steam installer runs. Sign in and install Deadlock from your Library, but do not launch it yet.
  4. Apply fixesQuit Steam and the runner, then reopen Deadlock Runner so the installed game and per-account settings are patched.
  5. PlayLaunch Deadlock in Steam. The runner reapplies its fixes whenever it starts.

02

What the runner carries forward

Known-good engine

Wine Sikarugir 10.0 revision 6 with ESYNC and MSYNC enabled.

DirectX 11

Deadlock is returned to DX11 when an update restores the Vulkan default.

Safe video start

Mac GPU identifiers and stable windowed settings are corrected without forcing a display resolution.

Controller repair

The Xbox mapping and controller glyph mode fixes are applied idempotently with backups.

03

Your Steam account stays yours

The download contains no login, userdata, installed game, or personal Steam library. A release-time privacy scan checks the app before the DMG is created. Steam and Deadlock come through Valve’s normal installer and client.

Before updating: Steam and game data live in Deadlock Runner.app/Contents/SharedSupport/prefix. Quit Steam and the runner, copy that folder out of the old app, install the update, and restore the backup before reopening Deadlock Runner.
